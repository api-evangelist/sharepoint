Today we announced the new Copilot app , bringing together how people assist, delegate, build, and automate work in one experience powered by Work IQ . Because Work IQ grounds these experiences in your work and organizational context, the quality and structure of knowledge behind them matters more than ever. The better that content gets, the better Copilot gets: in Microsoft evaluations, responses in the Copilot app improved by an average of 17 percentage points when the underlying content had been enriched and structured by Copilot .
