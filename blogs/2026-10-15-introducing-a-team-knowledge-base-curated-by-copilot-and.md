Collaboration is changing as teams of humans and agents now work together to get more done. That’s why we’ve evolved Copilot to empower these human-agent teams: just last month, we announced copilot​ in Teams, Autopilot as your digital teammate , and new tools in Copilot for managing a team’s content workflows . Today, we are taking the next step in human-agent team collaboration, designed to help address the problem of fragmented and outdated information and enable agent teammates to have the required team context they need to perform tasks and answer questions.
